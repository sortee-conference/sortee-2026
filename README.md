# SORTEE Conference 2026
*13th October 08:00 UTC to 14th October 9:15 UTC, 2026*

The Society for Open, Reliable, and Transparent Ecology and Evolution (SORTEE) Conference is an online forum featuring many interactive sessions, all about making research in fields related to ecology and evolutionary biology more open, transparent and reliable. 

Find conference resources and materials below.

Recordings of plenary talks, as well as opening and closing remarks, can be found on our [YouTube channel](https://youtube.com/@sortecoevo?si=V3kPEIzpiDx1-QeY).
